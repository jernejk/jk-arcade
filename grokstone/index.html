Don't Panic.

How the stone stands

  • Drag across the field and let go. Slide back to where you started to cancel. The mark is the first block you will hit.
  • You take a shot, then the rival answers.
  • A block stands on the block below it. Take that away and the courses above come down.
  • Gold stone, timber, and cinder kegs give up sooner than ashlar. The banner itself is only cloth.
  • Rubble from a fallen keep spends in the yard: thicker walls, and three more crews.
